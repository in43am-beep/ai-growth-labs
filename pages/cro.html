CRO FAQ

Get answers to the most common questions about our cro services.

CRO is the systematic process of increasing the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action - like calling your business, filling out a form, or booking an appointment. It focuses on improving user experience and removing conversion barriers.

Most businesses see a 20-50% improvement in conversion rates within the first 3 months. Even small improvements - like going from 2% to 3% conversion rate - can mean 50% more leads from the same traffic.

We use A/B testing, heatmap analysis, user session recordings, form optimization, page speed improvements, trust signal placement, CTA optimization, and mobile UX enhancements to systematically improve conversions.

Most businesses should optimize conversions first. Doubling your conversion rate has the same effect as doubling your traffic - but costs far less. We recommend CRO before scaling ad spend.

We track conversion rate, cost per lead, form submission rates, phone call volume, bounce rate, time on page, and revenue per visitor. Monthly reports show exactly how optimizations impact your bottom line.
