GBP Optimization FAQ

Get answers to the most common questions about our gbp optimization services.

We recommend weekly updates including Google Posts, photo uploads, and Q&A responses. Active profiles rank significantly higher than inactive ones in local search results.

GBP allows one primary category and up to 9 additional categories. Choose the most specific primary category for your business - this has the biggest impact on which searches you appear for.

Google Posts appear directly in your business listing and keep it fresh. Regular posts signal activity to Google, increase engagement, and can include CTAs that drive calls, bookings, and website visits.

Reviews are a top ranking factor for local SEO. Businesses with more positive reviews rank higher, get more clicks, and convert more visitors. Quality, recency, and response rate all matter.
