Social Media Content Calendar Template

April 10, 2026 · Social Media · 13 min read

Consistency is the #1 factor that separates successful social media accounts from dead ones. Yet most local businesses post sporadically — whenever inspiration strikes or when they remember. A content calendar solves this by planning your posts in advance, ensuring a strategic mix of content types, and maintaining the consistency that algorithms reward.

This guide provides a complete content calendar framework plus a downloadable template you can customize for your business in 2026.

Why You Need a Content Calendar

  • Consistency: Regular posting increases engagement by 40% and keeps you visible in algorithms
  • Strategy: Plan content around business goals, promotions, and seasonal trends
  • Efficiency: Batch-create content in advance instead of scrambling daily
  • Balance: Ensure a healthy mix of educational, entertaining, and promotional content
  • Accountability: Clear plan makes delegation and team collaboration easier

Content Calendar Framework

The 5-3-2 Rule

For every 10 posts, aim for 5 curated/educational posts that provide value, 3 original content pieces that showcase your expertise, and 2 personal/humanizing posts that show the people behind the business. This ratio keeps your feed engaging without being overly promotional.

Weekly Content Themes

Assign themes to each day to simplify planning:

  • Monday: Motivation/tips — start the week with value
  • Tuesday: Tutorial/how-to — teach something useful
  • Wednesday: Behind-the-scenes — show your team and process
  • Thursday: Testimonial/case study — social proof and results
  • Friday: Fun/engagement — polls, questions, or lighthearted content
  • Saturday: Community/local — highlight local events or partnerships
  • Sunday: Inspiration/preview — look ahead to the coming week

Platform-Specific Tips

Instagram

Post 3-5 feed posts per week, 5-10 Stories per week, and 1-2 Reels per week. Use high-quality visuals, relevant hashtags (mix of popular and niche), and engage with followers in comments and DMs. Reels currently get 2-3x more reach than static posts.

Facebook

Post 3-5 times per week. Focus on community engagement, local content, and events. Facebook Groups are highly effective for local businesses — consider creating one for your community. Use Facebook Events for promotions and in-person activities.

LinkedIn

Post 3-4 times per week for B2B businesses. Share industry insights, company news, team spotlights, and thought leadership content. LinkedIn articles and document posts (carousels) get exceptional organic reach compared to other platforms.

TikTok

Post 3-7 times per week. Focus on entertaining, authentic content that shows personality. Behind-the-scenes videos, day-in-the-life content, and quick tips perform best. TikTok rewards consistency and authenticity over production quality.

Content Creation Workflow

  1. Monthly planning (1 hour): Map out themes, promotions, and key dates for the month
  2. Weekly batching (2-3 hours): Create all content for the upcoming week in one session
  3. Scheduling: Use tools like Later, Hootsuite, or Buffer to schedule posts in advance
  4. Daily engagement (15-30 minutes): Respond to comments, messages, and engage with your community
  5. Weekly review (30 minutes): Analyze what performed well and adjust your strategy using analytics

Seasonal Content Planning

Plan content around seasonal trends and holidays relevant to your industry. HVAC companies should plan heating content for fall and cooling content for spring. Restaurants should plan around food holidays and local events. Gyms should capitalize on New Year's resolution season. Map out these seasonal opportunities at the beginning of each quarter.
