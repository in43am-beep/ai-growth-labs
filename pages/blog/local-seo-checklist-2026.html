Local SEO Checklist 2026

April 20, 2026 · Local SEO · 16 min read

Local SEO is the most important marketing channel for businesses that serve customers in a specific geographic area. When someone searches "dentist near me" or "best plumber in Austin," your local SEO determines whether they find your business or your competitor's. This comprehensive 2026 checklist covers everything you need to dominate local search results.

Google Business Profile Optimization

  • Claim and verify your Google Business Profile listing
  • Choose the most specific primary category for your business
  • Add all relevant secondary categories (up to 9 additional)
  • Write a keyword-rich business description using all 750 characters
  • Add your complete service list with descriptions
  • Upload 50+ high-quality photos (exterior, interior, team, work examples)
  • Add your service area or address accurately
  • Set accurate business hours including special hours for holidays
  • Enable messaging and add a booking link if applicable
  • Post weekly GBP updates (offers, events, news, tips)

On-Page SEO Checklist

  • Create unique title tags for every page with city + service keywords
  • Write compelling meta descriptions that include your location and a CTA
  • Use H1 tags with primary keyword on every page (one H1 per page)
  • Include your city/service area naturally in page content
  • Add LocalBusiness schema markup to your homepage
  • Add Service schema to each service page
  • Add FAQ schema to pages with FAQ sections
  • Embed a Google Map on your contact page
  • Include your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) on every page footer
  • Create individual pages for each service you offer
  • Create location-specific landing pages if you serve multiple areas

Technical SEO Checklist

  • Ensure your site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
  • Implement HTTPS security across all pages
  • Make your site fully responsive and mobile-friendly
  • Submit an XML sitemap to Google Search Console
  • Fix any crawl errors reported in Search Console
  • Implement proper canonical tags to avoid duplicate content
  • Optimize images with descriptive alt text and compressed file sizes
  • Ensure no broken links (404 errors) exist on your site
  • Set up proper redirects for any changed URLs

Citation and Directory Checklist

  • Ensure consistent NAP across all online directories
  • Claim listings on top directories: Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook
  • List on industry-specific directories relevant to your business
  • Claim local chamber of commerce and BBB listings
  • Submit to data aggregators (Factual, Neustar, Foursquare)
  • Audit and fix any inconsistent citations quarterly

Review Management Checklist

  • Set up a systematic process for asking happy customers for reviews
  • Respond to ALL reviews within 24 hours — both positive and negative
  • Aim for 5+ new Google reviews per month minimum
  • Diversify reviews across platforms (Yelp, Facebook, industry-specific sites)
  • Monitor your online reputation weekly using alerts
  • Never buy fake reviews or incentivize reviews with discounts

Content and Link Building Checklist

  • Publish 2-4 blog posts per month on topics your customers search for
  • Create comprehensive guides for your core services
  • Build backlinks from local organizations, sponsorships, and partnerships
  • Earn mentions through digital PR and community involvement
  • Create locally-focused content that references your community
  • Optimize for AI search and GEO to get cited by AI assistants

Tracking and Reporting

Use analytics tools to track your local search rankings weekly, Google Business Profile insights monthly, organic traffic from local searches, conversion rates from local visitors, and competitor performance in your market. Regular monitoring lets you identify what's working and what needs adjustment.
