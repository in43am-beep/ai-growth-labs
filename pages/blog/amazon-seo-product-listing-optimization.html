Amazon SEO: Product Listing Optimization

March 28, 2026 · E-Commerce · 14 min read

Amazon is the largest product search engine in the world — 63% of online shoppers start their product search on Amazon, not Google. If you sell products online, optimizing your Amazon listings is essential for visibility and sales. Amazon SEO follows different rules than Google SEO, and understanding the A9/A10 algorithm is key to ranking higher and selling more.

How Amazon's Search Algorithm Works

Amazon's algorithm prioritizes one thing above all: products that are most likely to generate a sale. Unlike Google, which aims to provide the best information, Amazon aims to make the most revenue. This means ranking factors are heavily weighted toward conversion data (sales velocity, conversion rate), relevance (keyword matching in titles and descriptions), availability (in-stock rate, fulfillment method), and customer satisfaction (reviews, ratings, return rate).

Product Title Optimization

Your product title is the most important ranking factor on Amazon. Follow these best practices: include your primary keyword early in the title, follow Amazon's title formula (Brand + Model + Product Type + Key Feature + Size/Color), keep titles under 200 characters for full visibility, use relevant keywords naturally without keyword stuffing, and capitalize the first letter of each word (Title Case).

Bullet Points and Description

Bullet Points (Key Features)

  • Use all 5 bullet points — each one is an opportunity for keyword inclusion
  • Lead each bullet with a benefit, followed by the feature that delivers it
  • Include secondary keywords naturally throughout your bullets
  • Address common customer questions and objections proactively
  • Keep bullets scannable — use CAPS for the benefit lead-in

Product Description

If you have A+ Content (Enhanced Brand Content) access, use it — A+ Content increases conversions by 5-10%. Include lifestyle images showing the product in use, comparison charts against competitors, brand story and trust signals, and additional keywords in the description text. For standard descriptions, write compelling HTML-formatted copy that includes relevant keywords and answers buyer questions.

Backend Keywords

Amazon provides hidden keyword fields that influence search visibility without cluttering your listing. Use all available characters (250 bytes), include synonyms, abbreviations, and alternate spellings, add Spanish translations if relevant to your market, don't repeat keywords already in your title or bullets, and separate keywords with spaces (not commas).

Image Optimization

  • Main image: Product on pure white background, filling 85% of the frame
  • Lifestyle images: Show the product being used in real-world contexts
  • Infographic images: Highlight features, dimensions, and included items
  • Comparison images: Show how your product compares to alternatives
  • Use all 7+ image slots — listings with 7 images convert significantly better
  • Include video — product videos increase conversion rates by 9-12%

Review Strategy

Reviews on Amazon are critical for both ranking and conversion. Use Amazon's "Request a Review" button for every order, enroll in Amazon Vine for new product launches, provide exceptional product quality and customer service, include helpful inserts that encourage reviews (without incentivizing), and respond to negative reviews professionally with solutions. Strong review management is just as important on Amazon as it is on Google.

Pricing and Competitive Strategy

Amazon customers are price-sensitive. Monitor competitor pricing regularly, use repricing tools to stay competitive, consider offering coupons and lightning deals for visibility boosts, and factor in FBA fees, advertising costs, and margin requirements when setting prices.

Amazon Advertising (PPC)

Amazon's advertising platform works similarly to Google Ads but within Amazon's ecosystem. Start with Sponsored Products campaigns targeting your main keywords, use auto-targeting campaigns to discover new keywords, graduate winning keywords to manual campaigns with optimized bids, and track your ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sale) — aim for 15-25% for profitable campaigns.
