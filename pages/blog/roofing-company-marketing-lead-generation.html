Roofing Company Marketing: Lead Generation Strategies

February 28, 2026 · Roofing Marketing · 14 min read

The Roofing Marketing Landscape

The US roofing industry is worth over $56 billion, with thousands of contractors competing for residential and commercial projects. 78% of homeowners search online before calling a roofer, and the companies that dominate local search capture the most valuable leads.

Roofing is a high-ticket service — the average roof replacement costs $8,000-$15,000. This makes every lead extremely valuable and justifies significant marketing investment. Local SEO, Google Ads, and reputation management are the three pillars of roofing lead generation.

Local SEO for Roofing Companies

When a homeowner discovers storm damage or a leak, they search "roofer near me" or "roof repair [city]." Local SEO ensures your company appears first.

Google Business Profile

Optimize your GBP:

  • List all roofing categories (roofing contractor, roof repair, gutter installation)
  • Add before/after photos of completed projects
  • Post storm damage tips and seasonal maintenance advice
  • Include your service area with all cities and zip codes
  • Collect reviews aggressively — aim for 15+ new reviews monthly

Service Pages That Rank

  • Roof replacement [city] — highest value keyword
  • Roof repair [city] — high urgency searches
  • Storm damage roof repair [city]
  • Commercial roofing [city]
  • Gutter installation and repair [city]
  • Roof inspection [city] — captures early-stage leads

Google Ads for Roofing

Google Ads is the #1 paid channel for roofing companies. The high average job value ($8,000-$15,000) makes even expensive clicks profitable.

  • LSAs: Google Guaranteed badge is essential. Cost per lead: $40-$80
  • Search Ads: Target "roof replacement," "roofer near me," storm-related keywords. CPC: $15-$40
  • Storm chasing campaigns: Activate emergency campaigns after severe weather events
  • Seasonal budgeting: Increase budgets 50-100% during peak roofing season (spring/summer)

Storm Damage Marketing

Storm events create surges in roofing demand. Prepared companies capture the majority of leads:

  • Pre-build storm damage landing pages ready to activate
  • Set up Google Ads campaigns that can be turned on within hours
  • Use geofencing to target storm-affected neighborhoods
  • Door-to-door canvassing combined with digital follow-up
  • Social media posts about free storm damage inspections

Reviews and Reputation

Reviews are crucial for roofing — it's one of the largest purchases homeowners make. They need to trust you completely.

  • 93% of homeowners read reviews before hiring a roofer
  • Companies with 200+ Google reviews dominate their markets
  • Include project photos in review responses when possible
  • Video testimonials from satisfied homeowners are extremely effective

Website Design for Roofers

Your roofing website needs to convert visitors into estimate requests:

  • Free estimate CTA on every page — prominent and easy to find
  • Project gallery — before/after photos organized by project type
  • Financing options — many homeowners need payment plans for roof replacement
  • Insurance claim assistance — highlight if you help with insurance claims
  • Manufacturer certifications — GAF, Owens Corning, CertainTeed certified

Content Marketing for Roofers

Content marketing educates homeowners and captures early-stage leads:

  • "How to know if you need a new roof" — captures research-phase searches
  • "Roof replacement cost guide [city]" — high-intent keyword
  • "How to file a roof insurance claim" — valuable for storm damage leads
  • "Asphalt shingles vs metal roofing" — comparison content ranks well

Measuring Roofing Marketing ROI

Track with analytics:

  • Cost per lead (target: $40-$120)
  • Lead-to-estimate conversion rate (target: 50-60%)
  • Estimate-to-job conversion rate (target: 30-40%)
  • Average job value by project type
  • Cost per acquisition (target: $200-$500)

Roofing marketing requires patience and consistency. Build your review profile, invest in SEO for long-term visibility, use Google Ads for immediate leads, and always be prepared for storm events. The roofing companies that market year-round — not just during peak season — are the ones that grow fastest.
