Restaurant Marketing Ideas That Increase Revenue

March 16, 2026 · Restaurant Marketing · 14 min read

Why Restaurants Need Digital Marketing

The restaurant industry is fiercely competitive, with over 1 million restaurants in the United States alone. 90% of diners research a restaurant online before visiting, making digital marketing essential for filling seats and driving delivery orders.

Restaurants that invest in local SEO, social media, and online reputation management see an average of 25-40% more foot traffic and online orders compared to those relying solely on word-of-mouth.

Local SEO for Restaurants

When someone searches "restaurants near me" or "best pizza in [city]," your restaurant needs to appear. Google Business Profile optimization is the most critical step.

GBP Optimization Checklist

  • Accurate hours — including holiday hours and special closures
  • Complete menu — upload your full menu directly to Google
  • High-quality food photos — restaurants with 10+ photos get 5x more engagement
  • Attributes — outdoor seating, delivery, dine-in, wheelchair accessible
  • Regular Google Posts — daily specials, events, seasonal menus

Website SEO

Your restaurant website should target location-based keywords:

  • "[cuisine type] restaurant in [city/neighborhood]"
  • "best [dish] near me"
  • "restaurant with private dining [city]"
  • "catering services [city]"

Social Media Marketing for Restaurants

Social media is arguably the most powerful marketing channel for restaurants. Food is inherently visual and shareable, making Instagram and TikTok perfect platforms.

Content Strategy

  • Professional food photography — the #1 content type for restaurant engagement
  • Behind-the-scenes kitchen content — shows authenticity and builds connection
  • Chef spotlights — humanize your brand
  • User-generated content — repost customer photos and reviews
  • Short-form video — food prep, plating, and ambiance videos for Reels/TikTok

Restaurants posting 4-5 times per week on Instagram see 3x more engagement than those posting once a week.

Online Ordering and Delivery Marketing

Online ordering now accounts for 30% of restaurant revenue on average. Whether you use third-party platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats) or your own ordering system, marketing these channels is critical.

  • Promote direct ordering on your website (save on commission fees)
  • Offer exclusive online-only deals to drive adoption
  • Optimize your menus on third-party platforms with great photos and descriptions
  • Use email marketing to promote online ordering to existing customers

Reputation Management for Restaurants

Online reviews make or break restaurants. A Harvard Business School study found that a one-star increase on Yelp leads to a 5-9% increase in restaurant revenue.

  • Monitor reviews on Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and Facebook daily
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours
  • Address negative reviews professionally and offer to make things right
  • Train staff to encourage happy diners to leave reviews
  • Use table tent cards or receipt messages with review QR codes

Email and SMS Marketing for Restaurants

Email marketing delivers the highest ROI for restaurants. Build your email list through:

  • WiFi login capture (require email to access restaurant WiFi)
  • Online ordering registration
  • Loyalty program sign-ups
  • Website popup offers (10% off first online order)

Effective Campaign Types

  • Weekly specials — feature new dishes and limited-time offers
  • Birthday/anniversary emails — personalized offers that drive visits
  • Event announcements — live music, wine dinners, holiday menus
  • Loyalty rewards — reward frequent diners with exclusive perks

Paid Advertising for Restaurants

Paid advertising can drive immediate traffic during slow periods or promote special events.

  • Google Ads — target "restaurant near me" and cuisine-specific searches
  • Facebook/Instagram Ads — use mouth-watering food photos to target local audiences
  • Geofencing — target people near your restaurant or near competitors
  • Retargeting — bring back website visitors who didn't place an order

Measuring Restaurant Marketing Success

Track these KPIs with analytics:

  • Covers/orders per day — broken down by channel
  • Online ordering revenue — track growth month over month
  • Review volume and average rating
  • Social media follower growth and engagement rate
  • Email open rates and click-through rates
  • Cost per customer acquisition by marketing channel

The restaurants that thrive are those that treat marketing as a core business function, not an afterthought. Start with the basics — great photos, an optimized Google profile, and consistent social media — then build from there.
