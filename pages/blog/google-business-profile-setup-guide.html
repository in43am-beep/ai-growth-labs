Google Business Profile: Complete Setup Guide

April 18, 2026 · GBP · 15 min read

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is arguably the single most important asset for local search visibility. It's what appears when customers search for your business name, when they look for services "near me," and when they browse Google Maps. A fully optimized GBP listing can generate 70% more visits and 50% more leads than an incomplete one.

This complete setup guide walks you through every step of creating and optimizing your Google Business Profile for maximum visibility and conversions in 2026.

Step 1: Claim and Verify Your Profile

If you haven't claimed your GBP yet, go to business.google.com and search for your business. If it exists, claim it. If not, create a new listing. Google will verify your ownership through a postcard, phone call, email, or video verification. Verification typically takes 3-14 days. Don't skip this step — unverified profiles have extremely limited visibility.

Step 2: Choose the Right Categories

Your primary category is the single most important ranking factor for Google Maps. Choose the most specific category that accurately describes your core business. Then add up to 9 additional secondary categories. For example, a dental practice might use "Dentist" as primary, with "Cosmetic Dentist," "Emergency Dental Service," and "Teeth Whitening Service" as secondary categories.

Step 3: Complete Every Section

  • Business name: Use your exact legal business name — no keyword stuffing
  • Address: Your exact physical address (or service area if you go to customers)
  • Phone: Your primary local phone number (not a call tracking number for GBP)
  • Website: Link to your homepage or a location-specific landing page
  • Hours: Set accurate regular hours AND special hours for all holidays
  • Description: Write a compelling 750-character description with natural keyword usage
  • Services: Add every service you offer with descriptions and pricing if possible
  • Attributes: Select all relevant attributes (women-owned, wheelchair accessible, etc.)

Step 4: Add High-Quality Photos

Businesses with 100+ photos get 520% more calls and 2,717% more direction requests than average. Upload photos of your storefront exterior (helps customers find you), interior (shows your environment), team members (builds personal connection), work examples (demonstrates quality), products or equipment, and your logo and cover photo. Add new photos weekly to signal an active, engaged business.

Step 5: Get and Manage Reviews

Reviews are the second most important local ranking factor. Actively ask satisfied customers to leave Google reviews. Respond to every review within 24 hours — positive reviews with genuine thanks, negative reviews with professional empathy and a resolution offer. Aim for 5+ new reviews per month. See our detailed guide on getting more Google reviews ethically. Strong reputation management directly impacts your GBP visibility.

Step 6: Post Regular Updates

Google Business Profile posts keep your listing fresh and engaging. Post weekly about special offers and promotions, events you're hosting or attending, new services or products, helpful tips related to your industry, team achievements or community involvement, and seasonal updates. Posts expire after 7 days, so consistency matters.

Step 7: Monitor Your Insights

GBP Insights shows how customers find and interact with your listing. Track search queries that trigger your listing, direct versus discovery searches, customer actions (calls, directions, website visits), photo views compared to competitors, and review trends over time. Use these insights to optimize your local SEO strategy and your overall analytics.

Common GBP Mistakes to Avoid

  • Keyword stuffing your business name — this violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension
  • Using a PO Box or virtual office — Google requires a real physical location
  • Ignoring negative reviews — unanswered complaints damage trust and rankings
  • Inconsistent NAP — your name, address, phone must match across all online listings
  • Setting and forgetting — GBP needs weekly attention to perform optimally
