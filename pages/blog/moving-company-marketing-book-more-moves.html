Moving Company Marketing: Book More Moves

February 22, 2026 · Moving Marketing · 13 min read

Marketing for Moving Companies

The US moving industry generates over $20 billion annually, with over 7,000 moving companies competing for customers. 83% of people searching for movers start with an online search, making digital marketing essential for booking more moves.

Moving is a high-stress, one-time purchase — customers research extensively before choosing a mover. Trust and reviews are the deciding factors in most cases. Local SEO and Google Ads capture customers at the moment of need.

Local SEO for Moving Companies

Local SEO drives the majority of moving leads. When someone searches "movers near me" or "moving company [city]," your company needs to appear in the top results.

Google Business Profile

Optimize your GBP:

  • List all service types (local moves, long-distance, commercial, packing)
  • Add photos of your trucks, team, and completed moves
  • Include service area — all cities and regions you cover
  • Post weekly with moving tips and promotional offers
  • Respond to every review promptly

Location-Specific Pages

Create pages targeting routes and destinations:

  • Movers in [city] — target each city you serve
  • [City A] to [City B] moving — target popular routes
  • Long distance movers from [city]
  • Commercial/office movers [city]
  • Storage solutions [city]

Reviews: The Trust Factor

Online reviews are absolutely critical for moving companies. People are trusting you with all of their belongings.

  • 95% of customers read reviews before hiring a moving company
  • Reviews mentioning "careful with belongings," "on time," and "fair pricing" are most persuasive
  • Companies with 100+ Google reviews get significantly more quote requests
  • DOT number and licensing should be prominently displayed

Google Ads for Movers

Google Ads is essential for moving companies during peak moving season (May-September).

  • LSAs: Licensed and insured badge builds trust. Cost per lead: $25-$55
  • Search Ads: Target "movers near me," "moving company [city]." CPC: $8-$25
  • Seasonal budgeting: Increase budget 75-100% during peak season
  • Long-distance campaigns: Separate campaigns for higher-value long-distance moves

Content Marketing for Movers

Content marketing captures people in the planning phase:

  • "Moving checklist" guides — high search volume, captures early-stage leads
  • "Cost of moving" calculators — tools that generate leads
  • Neighborhood guides — helpful for people moving to your area
  • Packing tips — demonstrates expertise and helpfulness
  • Moving with pets/kids guides — niche content that ranks well

Social Media and Email

Social media for movers focuses on trust-building:

  • Time-lapse videos of moves — satisfying to watch and share
  • Customer testimonials and thank-you posts
  • Moving tips and planning advice
  • Team spotlights showcasing experienced movers

Email marketing nurtures leads through the planning phase:

  • Moving timeline and checklist series
  • Quote follow-up sequences
  • Post-move feedback requests and review solicitation
  • Referral program promotions

Website Essentials

Your moving company website should generate quote requests:

  • Instant online quote form — the primary conversion point
  • DOT and MC numbers — displayed prominently for credibility
  • Insurance and licensing info — builds essential trust
  • Service descriptions — exactly what's included in each move type
  • FAQ section — address pricing, timing, and damage concerns upfront

Measuring Moving Company Marketing ROI

Track with analytics:

  • Cost per quote request (target: $25-$60)
  • Quote-to-booking conversion rate (target: 25-35%)
  • Average move value — local vs long-distance
  • Seasonal revenue trends — measure off-season marketing impact
  • Review generation rate — target a review from every completed move

Moving company marketing success depends on being visible when people need you and earning their trust quickly. Invest in reviews, local SEO, and Google Ads year-round — not just during peak season — and you'll build a steady pipeline of booked moves.
