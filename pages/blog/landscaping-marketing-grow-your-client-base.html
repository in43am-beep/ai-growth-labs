Landscaping Marketing: Grow Your Client Base

February 14, 2026 · Landscaping Marketing · 14 min read

Growing Your Landscaping Business

The US landscaping industry generates over $130 billion annually, with both residential and commercial segments experiencing strong growth. 74% of homeowners search online when looking for landscaping services, making digital marketing essential for growth.

Landscaping businesses that invest in local SEO, social media, and reputation management consistently outperform competitors relying on door hangers and yard signs alone.

Local SEO for Landscapers

Local SEO drives the majority of landscaping leads. Homeowners search "landscaper near me" or "lawn care [city]" when they need service.

Google Business Profile

Optimize your GBP:

  • List all services (lawn care, landscape design, hardscaping, irrigation, tree service)
  • Add seasonal photos of your best work — lush lawns, garden designs, patios
  • Post weekly with seasonal tips and project showcases
  • Include your complete service area
  • Collect reviews year-round

Service Pages

  • Lawn care and maintenance [city]
  • Landscape design [city]
  • Hardscaping and patios [city]
  • Irrigation installation and repair [city]
  • Tree trimming and removal [city]
  • Snow removal [city] (if applicable)

Social Media: Showcase Your Work

Social media is incredibly effective for landscaping because the work is inherently visual and impressive.

Content Strategy

  • Before/after transformations — the most engaging landscaping content by far
  • Time-lapse project videos — satisfying to watch and highly shareable
  • Seasonal lawn care tips — builds authority and engagement
  • Design inspiration — share landscape design ideas and trends
  • Drone footage — aerial views of completed landscape projects
  • Plant of the week — educational content about plants suited to your region

Instagram and Facebook are the primary platforms. Post 4-5 times per week during growing season.

Seasonal Marketing Strategy

Landscaping is seasonal — plan your marketing around the calendar:

  • Spring: Spring cleanup, lawn renovation, planting services
  • Summer: Lawn maintenance programs, irrigation services, hardscaping projects
  • Fall: Fall cleanup, aeration, overseeding, tree trimming
  • Winter: Snow removal, holiday lighting, spring planning consultations

Reviews and Reputation

Online reviews with photos of completed work are incredibly powerful for landscaping:

  • Reviews with project photos get 10x more engagement
  • Homeowners trust reviews from neighbors in their area
  • Aim for 5+ reviews per month during peak season
  • Ask clients to share photos of their completed project

Google Ads for Landscapers

Google Ads generates immediate leads during peak season:

  • Search Ads: Target "landscaper near me," "lawn care [city]." CPC: $3-$12
  • LSAs: Cost per lead: $15-$40
  • Seasonal budget increases: Ramp up in spring, maintain through summer
  • Snow removal campaigns: Separate winter campaigns if you offer snow services

Email Marketing

Email marketing converts seasonal customers into year-round maintenance clients:

  • Seasonal service reminders — "Time for spring cleanup!" "Schedule fall aeration"
  • Maintenance plan promotions — convert one-time customers to recurring contracts
  • Referral program — "Refer a neighbor, get a free mow"
  • Project inspiration — share design ideas to generate hardscaping/renovation leads

Website Design

Your landscaping website should showcase your work:

  • Project gallery — high-quality before/after photos organized by category
  • Free estimate form — the primary conversion point
  • Service descriptions with starting prices
  • Service area map
  • Team credentials — certifications, insurance, years of experience

Measuring Landscaping Marketing ROI

Track with analytics:

  • Cost per lead (target: $15-$50)
  • Lead-to-customer conversion rate
  • One-time to recurring client conversion rate (target: 25-35%)
  • Average annual client value — maintenance clients worth $2,000-$5,000/year
  • Seasonal revenue trends

Landscaping marketing success comes from showcasing beautiful work, building trust through reviews, and converting one-time project customers into recurring maintenance clients. Invest in your visual presence online and the leads will grow — just like the lawns you maintain.
